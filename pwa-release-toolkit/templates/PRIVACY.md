# Privacy Policy for **My PWA App**

**Last updated: October 2025**

This privacy policy describes how **My PWA App** ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the App. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide directly
- We do not require you to create an account.
- We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Location data:** If you grant permission, the App uses your device's GPS to calculate walking distance to nearby points of interest. This data is processed on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
- **Device information:** The App may collect anonymous device information (operating system version, screen size) to optimize the user experience. This information cannot be used to identify you personally.

### 1.3 Information we do NOT collect
- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
- We do not collect payment information (the App is free and has no in-app purchases).
- We do not collect analytics or tracking data.
- We do not share any information with third parties.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide the core functionality of the App (display nearby points of interest based on your location).
- To improve the App's performance and user experience.

## 3. How We Store Your Information
- All data collected by the App is stored locally on your device.
- We do not maintain any servers that store your data.
- Uninstalling the App removes all associated data from your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

## 5. Data Retention
Because all data is stored locally on your device, data is retained only as long as you keep the App installed. Uninstalling the App immediately deletes all data.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California), you may have rights including:
- The right to know what data we collect (this policy fulfills that right).
- The right to request deletion of your data (uninstall the App).
- The right to object to processing (uninstall the App).

Since we do not collect any data on our servers, these rights are automatically honored.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
