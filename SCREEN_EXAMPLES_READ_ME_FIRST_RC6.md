# RC6 simulated-screen policy

This release uses computer-generated, **simulated examples**, not verified screenshots of current Windows, Node.js, Excel, GitHub, Apple or Google interfaces. It preserves other preexisting diagrams, which are also illustrative unless otherwise specified.

New composites: `images/simulated_windows_excel_github.png` and `images/simulated_node_installation.png`. The composites appear in selected relevant chapters. **They are examples, not instructions to click controls which may not actually exist.**

Every chapter and appendix now includes a standing warning: match the *purpose* of the action, not the exact visual appearance, and stop if it cannot be identified. Account permissions, payment, credential and publishing steps require current official instructions. Never paste secrets into the workbook or images.

Coverage: 6 chapters received new simulated visual examples. This package does **not** include unique simulated screenshots for each of the 38 chapters or nine appendices. Further original examples and real-device verification remain open work.

No Windows Excel/VBA, native signing or app-store release certification is claimed. See toolkit inventory for gaps.
