## How to read the ledger
- `SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: a version- or policy-sensitive sentence was flagged for independent verification.
- `SOURCE_CANDIDATE_NOT_CERTIFIED`: topical source is provided, but no claim-by-claim certification is asserted.
- `NO_FACT_CHECK_PERFORMED`: ordinary prose or a statement not mapped to a current source.

The ledger is a comprehensive automated *inventory*, not a claim that every sentence was independently fact-checked. Verify costs, URLs, versions, store requirements, and native signing in the actual workflow before relying on the instructions.
